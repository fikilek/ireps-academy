# iREPS Academy Body of Knowledge V1

## Purpose

This document defines the broad structure of the iREPS Academy knowledge system.

iREPS Academy is not only a user manual. It is the full learning, support, training, and knowledge system for iREPS.

It will support:

- written manuals
- in-app help popups
- YouTube videos
- audio guides
- field training
- power systems learning
- media and evidence training
- troubleshooting
- assessments
- future accreditation-ready course packs

## Core principle

The iREPS Academy must become one source of truth.

The same approved knowledge should be reused for:

- User Manual pages
- app help popups
- course lessons
- video scripts
- audio scripts
- quick reference cards
- assessments
- support articles

## Main knowledge systems

1. iREPS Foundation
2. iREPS App User Manual
3. iREPS Data and Asset Knowledge
4. iREPS Workflows and TRNs
5. Power Systems and Energy Distribution
6. Fieldwork and Good Practice
7. Media and Evidence Academy
8. Maps and Location Knowledge
9. Roles and Responsibilities
10. Troubleshooting and Support
11. Assessment and Certification

## Build order

1. Define the Academy Body of Knowledge
2. Map all app pages and views that need help
3. Plan the User Manual
4. Plan courses and lessons
5. Plan video and audio learning
6. Plan assessments
7. Prepare accreditation-ready training packs
