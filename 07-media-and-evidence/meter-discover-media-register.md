# Meter Discovery media register

Status: **visual review draft, 23 September 2026**. Owner: iREPS Academy. Topic: [Meter Discovery placement examples](../01-body-of-knowledge/meter-discover-body-of-knowledge.md#placement-examples-context-and-closer-views).

## Origin and use

These eight image plates contain 16 **AI-generated realistic teaching views**, made with the built-in image_gen tool using the prompts preserved below. No reference photograph or production customer data was supplied to the generator. Each image visibly says **AI-generated teaching illustration**. None is a photograph of an actual iREPS visit or a verified installation.

The generated images were copied unchanged into Academy. Their purpose is placement recognition. Meter numbers, readings, model text, branding and municipal marks rendered by the model are illustrative details, not verified identifiers, specifications or endorsement. Do not use these plates as manufacturer identification references, installation instructions, safety-compliance evidence or customer evidence. Keep the origin label when reusing them in lessons or video scripts.

The contextual [outside-ERF site plan](meter-discover-outside-erf.svg) is an Academy-authored SVG. It illustrates two meter identities, two served premises and one physical kiosk location. It is conceptual and not to scale.

Actual field photographs remain a distinct future collection. Their register must identify the source, context, usage permission, any masking/redaction, and applicable release where a screen is shown. Do not relabel these generated plates as field photographs.
