# 03 · Replace meter: the removal, then the installation

**About 4 minutes.** For field workers who carry replacement meters.

**You need:** a DEV meter that is **Connected** and **prepaid**, and a meter number you can use for the new meter. The whole chain is recorded in one go — do not stop the recording between the forms.

---

## 1. Why this video (20 seconds)

**SAY:** A damaged or faulty meter has to come out and a new one goes in. From Sunday that is one instruction: Replace meter. The old meter comes out, and the app takes you straight to the form for the new one.

## 2. The finding (30 seconds)

**SHOW** the Anomaly boxes on an inspection.

**TAP** Meter Damaged, then Meter Button(s) Not Working. Take the anomaly photo.

**SHOW** the Normalisation ticks.

**TAP** Replace meter.

**SAY:** Meter Damaged or Meter Faulty: the work that follows is Replace meter. Tick it. No normalisation photo here — the removal and the installation carry their own.

**SAY:** No meter on the van? Then do not tick it. Pick the reason "No meter available to replace", and come back later.

## 3. Submit the inspection (25 seconds)

**TAP** SUBMIT INSP. **SHOW** the window: "Next: the removal form opens. The new meter goes in after it."

**TAP** SUBMIT. **SHOW** "Inspection sent — The removal form opens now. The new meter goes in after it."

**TAP** OK.

## 4. The old meter comes out (60 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the top of the removal form.

**SAY:** The instruction is already filled in and locked: Replace meter. Under it, From: Meter Inspection. And under the instruction the app tells you what happens: the new meter goes in, the installation opens after this.

**SHOW** the Remaining Credit box, with the meter still in place.

**SAY:** Open this form before you pull the meter out. On a prepaid meter it asks for Remaining Credit — the credit left on it — and a photo of the display. That photo is your before picture, and you can only take it while the meter is still working. If the display is broken, leave the credit blank and pick why: Display damaged.

**SAY:** A conventional meter asks for the Meter Reading instead, with the same kind of list when you cannot read it.

**SHOW** the section "Confirm meter removed".

**SAY:** Now take the meter out, and photograph it out of the wall. There is nothing to tick: sending this form is what confirms the removal. Safety confirmed is gone — that question no longer exists.

**TAP** the photo slot and take the photo.

**TAP** SUBMIT REMOVAL. **SHOW** the confirm window: "Next: Meter Installation opens."

**TAP** SUBMIT. **SHOW** "Removal sent — Meter … is removed. Meter Installation opens now."

**TAP** OK.

## 5. The new meter goes in (50 seconds)

**SHOW** the installation form.

**SAY:** Here is the new meter, at the same premise. It opened by itself: you did not go looking for it.

**SAY:** Fill it in the way you always do. The manufacturer starts empty now — pick the make on the meter in your hand.

**SHOW** the meter number, the manufacturer, the phase, the type.

**TAP** SUBMIT. **SHOW** MISSION SUCCESS.

## 6. What the office ends up with (30 seconds)

**SAY:** Three records, joined up: your inspection, the removal, and the installation. The old meter is Removed. The new meter is created — and like any new meter, it still needs COMM before it is commissioned.

**SAY:** If you take a meter out and put nothing back, pick Remove meter instead. The app says it under the choice: the meter is taken away, nothing follows.

**SAY:** And if you cannot get the meter out at all, do not open this form. Say so on the inspection — pick the reason there — and the office picks it up.

---

**Check before you send the video:** the locked instruction is readable, the Remaining Credit photo is taken before the meter comes out, and the recording never stopped between the three forms.
