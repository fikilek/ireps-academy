# 05 · When it will not send

**About 3 minutes.** For every field worker, and for supervisors. This is the video that saves the most work.

**You need:** a DEV meter, and a phone you can put into flight mode.

---

## 1. Why this video (20 seconds)

**SAY:** Work that stays on the phone is work nobody in the office can see. From Sunday the app tells you plainly when that happens, and where to go and fix it. Watch these windows.

## 2. The window that means it went (30 seconds)

**SHOW** an inspection being submitted, and the result window "Inspection sent".

**SAY:** This is the one you want: Inspection sent. Or Disconnection sent. Or Removal sent. If you did not see a window like this, it did not go.

## 3. No signal (45 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the phone going into flight mode. Fill in a short inspection and submit it.

**SHOW** the window "Offline: nothing was sent".

**SAY:** No signal when you press SUBMIT: nothing was sent. Press SAVE and it waits on the phone.

**SHOW** the window "Saved on this phone, not sent" where it appears.

**SAY:** And read this line: open it from Admin, Offline Submission Forms, and press SUBMIT. It does **not** send itself. Only a Meter Discovery does that.

**SAY:** If your finding needed a disconnection or a replacement, the window says that too: that work cannot start until this has been sent.

## 4. Where the work waits (40 seconds)

**SHOW** Admin, then Storage, then the screen headed **Offline Submission Forms**, with the saved form in it.

**SAY:** This is the place. The message says Admin, Offline Submission Forms — you get there through Admin, then Storage. Everything waiting is here. Open the form, press SUBMIT, and watch for the sent window.

**TAP** the form, submit it with the signal back on. **SHOW** the sent window.

**SAY:** Before you knock off, open this screen and make sure there is nothing left in it.

## 5. When the office refuses it (40 seconds)

**SHOW** a refusal, for example submitting an inspection with the finding not answered.

**SAY:** Refused means nothing was saved. The message says what is wrong. Fix it and submit again.

**SAY:** Some you can fix yourself: say what you did about the finding, take the photo, choose the status. Some are for the office: the meter is in another team's batch, or the meter record is wrong. Then report it with the meter number and carry on.

**SAY:** Every message is written out in the field guide, with what it means and what to do.

## 6. Close (15 seconds)

**SAY:** Three things. A sent window means it went. No window means it did not. And Admin, Offline Submission Forms, before you go home.

---

**Check before you send the video:** flight mode is visibly on when the offline window shows, and the Admin → Offline Submission Forms screen is on long enough to find it again.
