# 01 · Meter Inspection, start to end

**About 3 minutes.** For every field worker.

**You need:** a DEV meter that is **Connected** and **conventional**, at a premise you can reach on the app. iREPS must already hold a manufacturer and a placement for it, so that SAME has something to copy.

---

## 1. Why this video (15 seconds)

**SHOW** the meter list.

**SAY:** From Sunday you can inspect a meter yourself, without waiting for the office. This is the whole form, from the meter to the last window.

## 2. Starting it (20 seconds)

**SHOW** the meter card with its buttons.

**SAY:** Find the meter and press INSP. That is you starting the work in the field. If the office sent you the job instead, you accept it in My Work Orders and the same form opens.

**TAP** INSP. Wait for the form.

## 3. Access (15 seconds)

**SHOW** the Access Outcome buttons.

**SAY:** First, can you get to the meter? ACCESS YES carries on. NO ACCESS asks why and for a photo, and the meter is not changed.

**TAP** ACCESS YES.

## 4. SAME (40 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the top of Inspection Capture, with the empty boxes and the line above each one.

**SAY:** Every box starts empty. Above each box is what iREPS already holds. Look at the meter. If it is the same, press SAME and move on.

**TAP** SAME on Meter Number. **TAP** SAME on Manufacturer.

**SAY:** When iREPS holds nothing, SAME puts NAv. That is accepted; it means there is nothing recorded.

**SHOW** a box where the value differs. **TAP** DELETE, then pick the real value.

**SAY:** Different on the meter? DELETE, then put in what you really see. The app marks it DIFFERENT, and it will ask you to confirm it before it sends.

## 5. No breaker, no seal, no keypad (25 seconds)

**SHOW** the CB Size box.

**TAP** DELETE on CB Size. **SHOW** the list that appears.

**SAY:** CB size, seal number and keypad serial number work the same way. If there is none, clear the box and pick why — here, Circuit Breaker Missing.

**SAY:** And new from Sunday: if it is there, you photograph it. Record a CB size, a seal number or a keypad serial and a photo slot appears under it. If there is nothing there, nothing is asked for. CB size is typed in amps. The keypad serial is only asked on a prepaid meter.

## 6. What you found (30 seconds)

**SHOW** Current Status.

**SAY:** Current Status is what you see now: Connected or Disconnected. When you submit, the meter takes this status. If iREPS holds no status for the meter, this box starts empty and you choose.

**SHOW** the Anomaly and the detail, then Normalisation.

**SAY:** Then the anomaly, and its detail. This meter is fine, so Meter Ok, Operationally Ok, and Normalisation stays on None. If you fixed something on the spot — a keypad, a service point — tick it and photograph it.

## 7. The reading (15 seconds)

**SHOW** the Meter Reading box.

**SAY:** A conventional meter asks for the reading. Type it and photograph it, or say why there is none. A prepaid meter is not asked here.

## 8. Sending it (40 seconds) — the new part

**TAP** SUBMIT INSP.

**SHOW** the window that comes up, and stop there.

**SAY:** This is new. Before anything is sent, the app shows you what it is about to send: the meter, the status you found, the finding, and what opens next. Read it. GO BACK if it is wrong.

**SAY:** If something differs from iREPS, you get the differences window instead, and it lists every difference. Confirm only real differences, never a typing mistake.

**TAP** SUBMIT. **SHOW** the sending screen, then the result window. Stop on it.

**SAY:** And this is the one to remember. "Inspection sent" means it reached the office. If you do not see this window, it did not send.

**TAP** OK.

## 9. Close (15 seconds)

**SAY:** And one thing to know. What you confirm on this form is written to the meter itself. A meter that was recorded as illegally connected, and is fine now, reads fine on its card straight after your inspection. That is why the form shows you what iREPS holds and asks you to confirm anything different. Put in what is really at the meter.

**SAY:** That is the whole inspection. The next video shows what happens when you find a meter that is bypassed.
