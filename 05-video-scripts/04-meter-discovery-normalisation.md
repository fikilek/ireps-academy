# 04 · What is new on Meter Discovery

**About 2 minutes.** For every field worker. Everything else about Meter Discovery is unchanged, so keep this one short.

**You need:** a DEV premise with no meter on it, so you can capture a new one.

---

## 1. Why this video (15 seconds)

**SAY:** Meter Discovery works the way it always has. One part is new: what you do about what you found.

## 2. The list changed (40 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the Normalisation ticks on a Meter Ok capture.

**SAY:** With Meter Ok, nothing changes. None stays ticked, or tick what you fixed on the spot and photograph it.

**SHOW** the anomaly being changed to Illegally Connected, and the ticks changing with it.

**SAY:** Watch the list when the finding is not Meter Ok. None disappears. Now it is Disconnect meter, or Replace meter, or the reason you could not.

**SAY:** Illegally Connected means Disconnect meter. Meter Damaged or Meter Faulty means Replace meter. That is the rule, and it is the same on an inspection.

## 3. Wait for the meter (35 seconds)

**TAP** Disconnect meter, then submit the discovery.

**SHOW** the window that says "Preparing the disconnection…".

**SAY:** On a discovery the meter has to be created first, so the app tells you it is preparing the disconnection. It takes a few seconds. Wait for it.

**SHOW** the disconnection form opening, with the locked instruction.

**SAY:** Then the disconnection opens, already filled in: Illegal Connection, from Meter Discovery.

## 4. When there is no signal (25 seconds)

**SAY:** If you are offline, the meter is saved on the phone and sent when you have signal. The disconnection cannot start until the meter has been sent — the app says so.

**SAY:** When it has gone, open the meter and press DISC. You pick the instruction yourself that way.

## 5. Close (15 seconds)

**SAY:** If the app tells you the meter is not ready yet, nothing is lost. Open the meter and press DISC.

---

**Check before you send the video:** the list is on screen while the anomaly is changed, so the change can actually be seen.
