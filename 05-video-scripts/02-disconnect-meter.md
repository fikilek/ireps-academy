# 02 · Illegally Connected, and the disconnection that follows

**About 3 minutes.** For every field worker.

**You need:** a DEV meter that is **Connected**. You will record an Illegally Connected finding on it, so use a test meter, never a real customer's.

---

## 1. Why this video (15 seconds)

**SAY:** You find a meter that is bypassed. From Sunday, saying so is not the end of the job: the disconnection opens by itself and it is already filled in.

## 2. The finding (30 seconds)

**SHOW** the Anomaly boxes on an inspection.

**TAP** Illegally Connected, then Straight Connection (Meter Bypassed).

**SAY:** Anomaly: Illegally Connected. Then the detail — what you actually saw. Photograph it. The anomaly photo is required; without it the form will not go.

## 3. None is gone (40 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the Normalisation ticks, and the red line under them.

**SAY:** Look at the list. None is no longer there. When you find something other than Meter Ok, you either do the work or you say why you could not.

**SHOW** the line "This meter needs to be disconnected. Tick it, or say below why it was not done."

**TAP** Disconnect meter. **SHOW** "Disconnect meter recorded."

**SAY:** No photo needed here for the disconnection. The disconnection form carries its own proof.

## 4. Could not do it (30 seconds)

**TAP** Disconnect meter again to untick it. **SHOW** the reason list.

**SAY:** If you could not do it — the customer chased you away, it is not safe, the office told you to leave it — untick it and pick the reason. No photo for a reason. The finding is saved and nothing opens after it. Never leave it blank.

**TAP** Disconnect meter to tick it again.

## 5. Submit (25 seconds)

**TAP** SUBMIT INSP. **SHOW** the window.

**SAY:** The window tells you: next, the disconnection form opens.

**TAP** SUBMIT. **SHOW** "Inspection sent" and its line "The disconnection form opens now."

**TAP** OK.

## 6. The disconnection (45 seconds) — the important part

**SHOW** the top of the disconnection form. Stop on the instruction.

**SAY:** Look at the instruction: Illegal Connection, already filled in, and you cannot change it. Under it, From: Meter Inspection. That is how the office knows this disconnection came from what you found.

**SHOW** the level list.

**SAY:** What you choose is the level: Level 1, flip the circuit breaker. Level 2, remove the wire on the breaker. Level 3, remove the whole supply cable. Choose what you really did, and photograph it.

**TAP** a level, take the photo.

**TAP** SUBMIT DISCONNECTION. **SHOW** the confirm window, then "Disconnection sent — the meter is now Disconnected".

## 7. Two more things (25 seconds)

**SAY:** Two more. First: a meter can be off and still be stolen from, with the supply going around it. Record it the same way — the disconnection still opens, even on a meter that is already off.

**SAY:** Second: a disconnection you start yourself, with DISC on the meter, now needs its instruction. Credit Control Instruction, Illegal Connection or Non Payment. Pick one, and it will go through.

---

**Check before you send the video:** the locked instruction and the From line are readable, and all three levels were on screen.
