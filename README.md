# iREPS Academy

iREPS Academy owns the platform's training knowledge: its master dictionary, user manuals, in-app help content, courses, video and audio scripts, field guidance, assessments and support material. It also develops broader utility and municipal asset, revenue-protection and revenue-management education.

**Workstream owner:** the owner's iREPS Academy Codex task. Other agents and contributors submit evidence and proposed changes through the [contribution process](00-academy-governance/CONTRIBUTING.md). The owner retains product and policy decisions.

## Current maturity

This consolidation establishes ownership and preserves source material. It is not a publication or a certification of the mobile/web applications. Imported guides remain drafts until their instructions, permissions and messages are verified against a named release. QA and Trials are planned. Meter reading needs further strengthening before production. The common offline system and permission inheritance are unresolved.

Application status and content status are recorded separately in the [content register](00-academy-governance/CONTENT_REGISTER.csv). No imported guide is marked published by this work.

## Sources and recovery

- [Source register](00-academy-governance/SOURCE_REGISTER.csv): original location, branch, commit and SHA-256 where available.
- [Migration record](00-academy-governance/MIGRATION_RECORD.md): preservation, redirects and limitations.

Engineering rules, database schemas, application runtime code, tests and deployment records remain owned by their application repositories. Academy owns their learner-facing explanation and references the technical sources.
