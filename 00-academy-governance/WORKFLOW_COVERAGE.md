## 25 September form coverage update

The table above preserves the earlier 23 September assessment. Its Gap/Partial learning statuses for forms are superseded by the [41-family module inventory](FORM_MODULE_INVENTORY.csv): five-component draft packages now exist for authentication, premises, installation, commissioning, reading, inspection, disconnection/reconnection/removal, No Access and administrative/operational forms. Release acceptance and individual field/error semantics remain review work. Procurement, physical stores, retirement/disposal and vending have explicitly proposed packages. QA, Trials and full billing retain their previous product statuses.
